1DID YOU KNOW

Apples are fruits.

However, all fruits form from inside the ovary of a flower. Fruits such as pumpkins, tomatoes, beans all have seeds. If it’s not a fruit, it’s the vegetable part of the plant (celery, potatoes, lettuce).

Flowers can be female, male, or BOTH! The pollen from the male flower is carried to the female flower by bees to pollinate it.
