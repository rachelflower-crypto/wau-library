Every month, readers post me the small secrets of their private lives: the rituals, the rebellions, the habits they’d never mention at a dinner party. Some are hilarious. Some are tender. All of them are anonymous, and all of them are welcome. Here are three to begin with.

Your turn, if you like

Who are you when the door is shut? Tell me. I’ll take out anything that could identify you, and I won’t publish a word without your say-so.

Before you confess

  • Your confession is anonymous. Please leave out names and places.
  • The Librarian may edit it for length and clarity.
  • Anything that could identify you, or anyone else, will be removed.
  • You can withdraw it at any time before it’s published.
