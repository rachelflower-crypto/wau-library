Dear you, I’m writing from a little further along. Not far. Far enough to see the path behind me, which is more than you can manage from where you’re standing, so do trust me on a few things.

The thing you’re dreading on Thursday goes fine. Not brilliantly. Fine. You will spend more energy dreading it than doing it, and I’d quite like that energy back, if you don’t mind.

You were right to stop apologising for taking up room in meetings. You were wrong about the fringe. We’ll say no more.

She’s not wrong. — L.
I am not a better version of you. I’m the same woman, with more room.

I haven’t got everything sorted. I want to be clear about that, because I know you’ve been picturing me in linen, serene, with a morning routine. I still lose my keys. I still say yes when I mean maybe. But I laugh sooner now, and I leave sooner too, and both have been a tremendous relief.

Here is the only advice I’m permitted to send back: keep a shelf clear. Don’t fill every hour, every plan, every version of who you’re meant to be. I’ll need somewhere to put my things when I arrive.

Reply by return of post. Or don’t. She already knows.
